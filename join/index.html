Questions

        
Good to know.

      

      

        
Is Bejustt an official organisation?
Not yet. We are a student community in our founding period, built on social media. We say this openly because we believe in starting honestly.

        
Who can join?
Law students from any country who want to contribute as country representatives, writers, interviewers or members of our academic team.

        
How much time does it take?
As much as you can give. Everything is designed to fit alongside your studies.

        
Which language do we work in?
Our main working language is English. This website is available in eight languages.

        
How do I apply?
Fill in the short form on this page, or message us on Instagram at @bejustt.law.
