How to install

iPhone and iPad

  1. 1Open this page in Safari and tap the Share button.
  2. 2Scroll down and choose “Add to Home Screen”.
  3. 3Tap “Add”. Bejustt appears on your home screen.

Android

  1. 1Open this page in Chrome and tap “Install the app”.
  2. 2Or open the ⋮ menu and choose “Install app”.
  3. 3Bejustt appears with your other apps.

Mac and PC

  1. 1In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar.
  2. 2In Safari on Mac, choose File → Add to Dock.
  3. 3Bejustt opens in its own window.
